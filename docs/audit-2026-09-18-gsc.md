# fowned.live audit, 2026-09-18

Instruments: Google Search Console via GSC Wizard (`sc-domain:fowned.live`,
90 days through 2026-09-15, ClickHouse warehouse) and a raw-HTML `curl` pass.
No rendered-markdown fetch was used for any "missing X" conclusion.

## Correction to the prior audit

An earlier pass read `~/Projects/business/fowned-website/` and
`~/Projects/_fowned-upstream/`, concluded the property was a single
`index.html` with no meta description and no structured data, and called that
a dogfooding failure. **That conclusion was wrong.** Both directories are
stale clones stopped at commit `47b2974` (2026-08-13). The deployed source is
`~/Projects/fowned-web`, 27 page modules behind a static build. The live site
has titles, meta descriptions and structured data on every page checked.

## What Search Console actually shows

90 days: **237 impressions, 0 clicks, average position 34.5.** Prior period
zero, so the property began appearing inside this window.

The 16 named queries sum to 218 of 237 impressions, a 92 percent coverage
ratio. That is human traffic, not scraper noise, so the zero-click figure is
a real signal rather than an artifact.

The impressions split cleanly in two, and the halves need opposite treatment:

| Cluster | Pages | Impressions | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Non-brand demand | `/guides/*` | 185 | 72 to 80 |
| Brand-shaped | `/`, `/about/`, `/terms/`, `/how-it-works/` | 76 | 2.7 to 7.9 |

**Non-brand cluster.** Google has mapped the guides to the right demand
(`chatgpt tracking`, `geo for local business`, `aeo for local businesses`) and
ranks them nowhere. Position 72 to 80 is not a click-through problem and no
on-page edit reaches it. The missing input is off-site authority.

**Brand cluster, and the finding that changes strategy.** The homepage sits at
position 2.9 for the query `fowned` across 31 impressions and earns zero
clicks. A business ranking third for its own name does not get zero clicks
from people looking for it. The likeliest reading is that `fowned` is being
searched as internet slang, not as this company, and the site is an
irrelevant result those searchers correctly skip.

That matters commercially: the brand term carries no acquisition value and
brand-CTR work on it would optimize for an audience that does not want the
business. Treat these impressions as noise in the denominator.

## Technical state

`audit_onpage_seo` over four pages: **0 critical, 0 high, 1 medium, 3 low.**
All four indexable, all self-canonical, response times 47 to 115 ms.

- FIXED, medium: `Organization` missing required `logo`. Commit `7754e08`.
- OPEN, low: two guide titles at 71 and 75 characters may truncate. Not
  scheduled, because truncation costs nothing at position 72.
- NOT A DEFECT: the audit counted `images/fowned-portrait-transparent.png` as
  missing alt text. Its `alt=""` is a deliberate decorative marker and is
  correct. Changing it would make screen readers announce a decorative image.

## What the constraint is

Content quality and technical quality are not the limiting factor and further
work on either will not move clicks. Every non-brand query the site appears
for sits at position 64 or worse. The constraint is off-site authority:
nothing yet cites, links to or mentions this business.

## Ranked work

1. Off-site authority for the two guides already mapped to real demand. This
   is the only item that can move the non-brand cluster.
2. Decide on the brand-term collision. Either accept that `fowned` is
   unwinnable as an acquisition query, or change how the business is named in
   search. This is an operator call, not an engineering one.
3. Nothing else. Adding pages before authority exists produces more results at
   position 80.

## Provenance and limits

Search Console figures are Verified, read this date from the cited property.
The slang-collision reading of the zero-click brand term is **Inferred**: it
explains the data but was not separately tested. A search-results check for
`fowned` would confirm or kill it and has not been run.
